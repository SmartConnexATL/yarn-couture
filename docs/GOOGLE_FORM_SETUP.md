# Google Form Setup Guide for Yarn Couture Website
**Step-by-Step Instructions**

---

## What Is This?
A **Google Form** is a free tool that collects customer inquiries and emails them to you automatically. When someone fills out the form on your website, you'll get an email notification and their response will be saved in a spreadsheet.

**Time to set up:** 10-15 minutes  
**Cost:** Free

---

## STEP 1: Create a New Google Form

1. Go to **[Google Forms](https://forms.google.com)** (in your browser)
2. Click the **+ (Plus icon)** or **"Create a new form"**
3. Name your form: **"Yarn Couture Inquiry Form"**
4. Add a description (optional): **"Get in touch to ask about our yarns"**

---

## STEP 2: Add Form Questions

### Question 1: Name
1. Click **"Untitled Question"** and type: **"What's your name?"**
2. Change type to: **"Short answer"**
3. Toggle **"Required"** ON (so people must fill it in)

### Question 2: Email
1. Click **"+"** to add another question
2. Type: **"Email address"**
3. Change type to: **"Email"** (not short answer!)
4. Toggle **"Required"** ON

### Question 3: Message/Inquiry
1. Click **"+"** to add another question
2. Type: **"What would you like to know?"** or **"Tell us about your inquiry"**
3. Change type to: **"Paragraph"** (allows longer text)
4. Toggle **"Required"** ON

### Question 4 (Optional): How did you hear about us?
1. Click **"+"** to add another question
2. Type: **"How did you hear about us?"**
3. Change type to: **"Multiple choice"**
4. Add options:
   - Instagram
   - Facebook
   - Google Search
   - Festival/Market
   - Word of Mouth
   - Other

---

## STEP 3: Customize the Form Look (Optional)

1. Click the **paintbrush icon** (top right)
2. Choose a color theme (suggest: Dark/gold to match your brand)
3. Upload a header image (optional—your yarn photo?)
4. Click **"Save"**

---

## STEP 4: Set Up Email Notifications

1. Click the **settings icon** (gear icon, top right)
2. Go to **"Notifications"** tab
3. Toggle **"Email notifications for new responses"** ON
4. Make sure email is set to: **itsyarncouture@gmail.com**
5. Click **"Save"**

**Now you'll get an email every time someone submits the form!**

---

## STEP 5: Get the Embed Code

This is how we add the form to your website so people can fill it out without leaving your site.

1. Click the **"Send"** button (top right of form)
2. Click the **"Embed"** icon (looks like `</>`—third option)
3. Copy the code (click **"Copy"**)
4. **Save this code somewhere** (you'll give it to me to add to your website)

**Example of what it looks like:**
```html
<iframe src="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd..." ...></iframe>
```

## STEP 6: Test Your Form

1. **Close the editor** by clicking the **"X"**
2. Click **"Preview"** (eye icon)
3. Fill out the form as if you're a customer
4. Submit it
5. Check your email (itsyarncouture@gmail.com) — you should get a notification!
6. Go back to your form and click **"Responses"** tab—you should see your test submission

**If it works, you're done!** 🎉

## What Happens Next?

**After the form is on your website:**
1. Customer fills it out
2. They click "Submit"
3. You get an email notification
4. Response appears in Google Sheets (auto-created)
5. You can reply to their inquiry

---

## Where to Find Responses

All form submissions are automatically saved to a **Google Sheet**.

**To view responses:**
1. Open your form
2. Click **"Responses"** tab (top)
3. Click the **Sheet icon** (looks like a table)
4. You'll see all submissions organized by date

## Troubleshooting

**Q: I don't see an email notification**
- A: Check your spam/junk folder
- Make sure notifications are turned ON in form settings

**Q: The form isn't showing on the website**
- A: Make sure the embed code was added correctly
- Check that no extra characters were added/removed

**Q: I want to edit the form after it goes live**
- A: You can! Any changes update automatically on the website
