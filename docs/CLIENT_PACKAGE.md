## 💡 Pro Tips for Client

✅ **Do This:**
- Take photos in natural light (window light is best)
- Use simple, clean backgrounds
- Show yarn color clearly
- Take multiple angles
- Test Google Form before sending code

❌ **Avoid:**
- Harsh shadows or overly dark lighting
- Cluttered backgrounds
- Blurry or low-quality phone photos
- Artificial/fluorescent lighting (changes yarn colors)
