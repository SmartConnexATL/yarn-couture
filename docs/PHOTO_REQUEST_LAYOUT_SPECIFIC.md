## 📸 How to Shoot Photos at Home

### Lighting Tips
- Use **natural window light** (best time: morning or late afternoon)
- Avoid harsh midday sun (creates shadows)
- White/light backgrounds help colors pop

### For Yarn Photos
- Place yarn on a clean surface (white sheet, wooden table, neutral fabric)
- Take multiple angles (overhead, 45°, close-up)
- Try grouping colors together for visual interest

### For Finished Pieces
- Drape on a hanger, mannequin, or hold in natural light
- Show the texture and colors clearly
- Include the maker (your hands, body) in frame if possible

### For Product Shots
- Simple, clean background
- Consistent lighting across all product photos
- Show color accurately (take in daylight, not artificial light)
